## Research

I am a theoretical physicist working on holography and quantum field theory.<br>
My research includes supersymmetric supergravity solutions and gravitational observables in AdS/CFT, as well as the symmetries, and dynamics of five-dimensional quantum field theories.<br>
More recently, I have been developing localization methods in supergravity to extract exact results without requiring explicit solutions to the equations of motion.
